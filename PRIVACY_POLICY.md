# Privacy Policy for Compose Calculator

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Compose Calculator** (referred to as "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by **Jesus Daniel Medina Cruz**, handles your information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Compose Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- **No Personal Information:** We do not require, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.
- **Local On-Device Processing:** All calculations and user interactions occur entirely on your local device. We do not transmit calculation history, mathematical expressions, or results to any external servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.
- **No User Tracking or Profiling:** We do not track user behavior, create user profiles, or use advertising identifiers (AAID / IDFA).

## 2. Device Permissions

The Application requires **no sensitive device permissions** to operate:
- No camera or microphone access
- No location access (coarse or fine)
- No access to contacts, photos, media, or external storage
- No background tracking or notifications

## 3. Third-Party Services and Libraries

The Application is built using open-source Kotlin Multiplatform technologies. 
- The production version does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking tools, or behavioral measurement SDKs.
- No personal user data is shared with or sold to third parties.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Because the Application does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age.

## 5. Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. Because all calculations and state management are executed locally within the Application's sandbox on your device, your input remains confidential and under your direct control.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically for any changes.
