A straightforward, fast, and distraction-free calculator.

Designed for everyday math without pop-up ads, laggy interfaces, or unnecessary permissions.

Why Choose Compose Calculator?

• Zero ads, zero sign-ups: No banners blocking your buttons, no popups, and no accounts required. Open the app and calculate.
• 100% offline & private: Never asks for camera, contacts, storage, or location access. Every calculation runs locally in your device memory.
• Exact decimal precision: Avoids standard floating-point rounding errors (like 0.1 + 0.2) by using high-precision decimal arithmetic.
• Clean & modern design: Built with Material You (Material 3) styling, comfortable touch targets, full edge-to-edge support, and seamless dark mode.
• Daily essentials: Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages, smart automatic parenthesis balancing, and undo button.
• Lightweight: Minimal app size with zero background battery drain.

Open Source
Built using Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform. Clean, transparent, and open source on GitHub.
