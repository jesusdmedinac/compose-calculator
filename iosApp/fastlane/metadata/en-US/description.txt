A straightforward, fast, distraction-free calculator built for iPhone.

Liquid Glass keys that respond to your touch, digits that roll as they change, and a background in the app's colors. No ads, no accounts, no internet connection needed.

• No ads, no sign-ups: open the app and calculate.
• 100% offline and private: never asks for your contacts, location, photos or files. Every calculation runs on your iPhone.
• Exact decimal precision: avoids common rounding errors like 0.1 + 0.2.
• The essentials: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, automatic parentheses and step-by-step delete.
• Your last calculation always in view.

Open source
Compose Calculator is built with Kotlin Multiplatform and SwiftUI. The source code is open on GitHub:
https://github.com/jesusdmedinac/compose-calculator
